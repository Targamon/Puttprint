Putt Print — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. Draft — production host TBD.

What we collect

  • Profile: the display name and dot color you choose. No email or password is required.
  • Shot locations: GPS coordinates captured only when you tap PLAY to stamp a shot, plus the club you pick.
  • Rounds and learned club distances derived from your stamped shots.
  • Courses you add (name, location, holes) — shared with all players.

How we use it

To show your ghosts (Last / Best / Perfect), sync multiplayer rooms, and learn your club distances. We do not sell data, show ads, or track location in the background.

Where it lives

On your device (offline cache) and on the Putt Print server. Multiplayer room members see your name, color and shot dots for that room.

Deletion

Email the support contact below to delete your account and rounds. Clearing app data removes the local copy.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.
