FAQ

GPS says unavailable?
Allow location for Putt Print. In a browser, the page must be HTTPS. Use "Simulate GPS" when you are not at the course.
How do I play with friends?
Host taps Play with friends → Host and shares the 4-letter code; others tap Join on their own phones.
When do my club distances update?
After a completed round, once a club has at least 3 full-swing shots logged.
My course isn't listed
Use + Add course; it is shared with every player.
